## Choose a production technique

The grouping and the following session prompts are editorial guidance, not additional artist statements.

- **[Capture & commit](#capture):** Decide what the performer needs to hear, what to record and what to commit while the idea is working.
- **[Build a groove](#groove):** Establish the pulse and interaction first, then add or remove rhythmic detail against that foundation.
- **[Shape a sound](#sound):** Name the change you want in the sound, then test the smallest useful change in synthesis, filtering or processing.
- **[Create space](#space):** Choose what should feel close, distant or wide; judge the effect alongside the lead and the rest of the arrangement.
- **[Control dynamics](#dynamics):** Identify the moment whose impact is wrong, then compare local automation or envelope changes in context.
- **[Choose & arrange](#decisions):** Give each part a role and compare alternatives against the song’s intention before adding more material.
