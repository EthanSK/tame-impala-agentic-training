## Transcript archive and accuracy

Private research copies contain one Markdown file per transcribed/captioned video or episode, with the original source link, method, timing basis and limitations at the top. Supplied course VTT files, YouTube caption exports, local podcast ASR and the publisher transcript are preserved without silently repairing the originals. The archive is separate from the public repository; public per-source files contain original paraphrased notes, not full third-party transcripts, lyrics or media.

Captions and ASR contain errors in names and speaker turns. First-hand identifies the person whose account is summarized, not a certified transcript. Broken Record’s export collapses speaker labels, so surrounding questions determine attribution; Rubin’s advice about other artists is not presented as Parker’s own method. Complete human listening and frame-by-frame equipment audits have not been performed.

## Adding coverage

Use an authorized source or publisher-provided transcript. Record the source URL, date, edition, timestamp basis, retrieval method and scope. Review uncertain names against audio or visible equipment when available. Add concise original claims with stable IDs, or cross-link the existing claim when the same interview repeats. Preserve unreviewed and gated candidates so a future agent can continue rather than mistake missing access for missing information.
