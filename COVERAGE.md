## Transcript policy

Public per-source Markdown files contain original structured notes, not full third-party transcripts. Supplied course VTT captions are preserved without silent correction in the private archive, alongside one timestamped Markdown file per part. Those files are outside the public repository and site. Public podcast transcription is an internal research aid only; music, lyrics and advertisements are excluded from the notes.

The course captions contain mistakes in names and speech. Public YouTube captions similarly misspell Vermona and garble some phrasing. The podcast ASR confuses song names and equipment in places. First-hand indicates the speaker, not transcript accuracy. Record corrections with evidence and retain the original uncertainty.

## Timing

## Adding the missing coverage

Start with an authorized source or publisher-provided transcript. Record the source URL, date, exact edition, local timestamps, retrieval/inspection method and scope. Review uncertain names against audio or visible equipment. Add concise original claims with stable IDs, or link to an existing claim when the edit repeats the same interview. Do not count a teaser as independent confirmation of its parent episode.
